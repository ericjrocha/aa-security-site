About us

The family behind AA Security.

From the badge to the boardroom

Martha Zamudio

Founder and CEO

Martha Zamudio spent her career in law enforcement as a Chicago Police Officer before bringing that experience to Arizona. She is a licensed private investigator with a master's degree in criminal social justice and more than 20 years in the field.

She started AA Security in 2017 and named it after her twin sons, Alan and Austin, who work alongside her today. Every client gets the same standard she held on the street: show up, pay attention, and protect people like they're family.
